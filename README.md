## ◈ &nbsp;01 — ABOUT ME

<img align="right" width="38%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jaison-68/jaison-68/main/assets/about_card.png" alt="Abstract data analytics artwork">

I'm a **PGDM candidate in Business Analytics at MCC Boyd-Tandon School of Business**, with a B.Com. background and experience spanning business operations, marketing analytics, automation and web development.

I like working on problems where the answer isn't obvious at first: inconsistent data, weak conversion funnels, manual reporting, or digital journeys that make things harder than they should be.

- 🔎 **Analyse:** KPI reporting, funnel diagnosis, unit economics and dashboards
- ⚙️ **Automate:** Python, MySQL, APIs, scraping and n8n workflows
- 🧩 **Build:** business websites, e-commerce experiences and lead-capture systems
- 🎯 **Focus:** turning analysis into a decision, an improvement or a working product
