## 01 — A little about me

<img align="right" width="36%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jaison-68/jaison-68/main/assets/about_card.png" alt="Abstract analytics artwork representing data, dashboards, and business decisions">

I'm **Jaison**, a PGDM candidate in Business Analytics at **MCC Boyd-Tandon School of Business**. My background is in commerce, business operations, marketing analytics, and building digital products.

I like taking a business problem from the messy first version to something people can use: a clearer report, an automated workflow, a better customer journey, or a decision grounded in the numbers.

- **Analyse:** campaign funnels, KPIs, unit economics, and dashboards
- **Automate:** recurring reports, data flows, and lead follow-up
- **Build:** websites, e-commerce experiences, and practical business tools
